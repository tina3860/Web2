API Data Integration

Integrated 15 third-party APIs enabling real-time data consolidation across platforms

The Business Problem

The organization used 15 different SaaS platforms for CRM, marketing, payment processing, and customer support, but data existed in silos. Marketing couldn't see customer support history, sales lacked marketing engagement data, and executives had no unified customer view.

Manual data exports were error-prone and infrequent. Real-time insights required automated data consolidation.

Findings

Platform audit discovered:

  • Each system had different customer identification schemes requiring mapping
  • APIs had varying authentication mechanisms and rate limit constraints
  • Data inconsistencies requiring validation and reconciliation
  • Historical backfill needed for 2 years of customer interaction data

Results

  • Successfully integrated 15 third-party APIs into unified data platform
  • Created customer identity mapping with 99.2% accuracy
  • Implemented incremental sync reducing processing load by 75%
  • Backfilled 2 years of historical transaction data (8.2 million records)
  • Built centralized API management layer handling authentication and rate limiting
  • Enabled real-time customer data available to all internal systems

Immediate Impact on Operations

Customer support teams can now see complete purchase history and past interactions in real-time. Sales team has visibility into customer marketing engagement enabling more targeted outreach. Marketing department can track customer lifecycle across all touchpoints.

"The API integration project gave us a unified view of our customers for the first time. Our teams can now collaborate better and customers receive more relevant interactions across all touchpoints."

Chief Information Officer Mid-Size SaaS Company
