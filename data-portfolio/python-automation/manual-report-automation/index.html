Manual Report Automation

Automated 12 manual reports freeing up 960 hours of analyst time annually

The Business Problem

The analytics team spent 80 hours monthly compiling data from multiple systems into Excel reports. These manual processes were error-prone and delayed critical stakeholder reporting. Key reports took 2-3 days to prepare, reducing team capacity for strategic analysis.

Leadership wanted to reclaim analyst time from manual data compilation for higher-value work.

Findings

Process analysis revealed:

  • 12 recurring reports following predictable data transformation patterns
  • Manual data extraction and concatenation from 8 different systems
  • 5 reports requiring version control due to formula changes
  • Approximately 960 analyst hours annually spent on report generation

Results

  • Automated all 12 reports through Python scripts with scheduled execution
  • Reduced report generation time from 80 to 5 hours monthly (94% reduction)
  • Implemented automated quality checks reducing errors by 87%
  • Freed up 960 hours annually of analyst capacity
  • Created version-controlled report templates for consistency
  • Enabled daily report generation vs. weekly manual process

Immediate Impact on Operations

The team reclaimed 40 hours monthly for strategic analysis projects. Report turnaround improved from 2-3 days to same-day delivery. Error rates in reports dropped to near zero as automated validation caught inconsistencies.
