Business Logic Automation

Automated complex pricing logic reducing manual calculation errors by 89%

The Business Problem

The pricing team manually calculated complex pricing tiers for 500+ product combinations monthly, applying discounts based on volume, customer type, seasonal factors, and promotional campaigns. Manual calculations were error-prone and consumed 120 hours monthly.

Pricing errors led to disputed invoices and lost revenue. The process couldn't scale as product offerings expanded.

Findings

Process documentation revealed:

  • Complex multi-factor pricing logic with 8 conditional branches
  • Seasonal adjustments requiring manual override of base prices
  • Customer-specific contracts with unique pricing agreements
  • Invoice generation errors traced to manual pricing step in 12% of cases

Results

  • Developed Python pricing engine with all business logic codified
  • Reduced pricing calculation time from 120 to 8 hours monthly (93% reduction)
  • Eliminated manual calculation errors (89% reduction in disputed invoices)
  • Enabled dynamic pricing supporting 50 simultaneous promotional campaigns
  • Created audit trail for all price changes and adjustments
  • Integrated with invoicing system for straight-through processing

Immediate Impact on Operations

Pricing team reclaimed 110 hours monthly for strategic pricing optimization. Finance team experienced 89% reduction in incorrect invoices, improving customer relationships and reducing dispute resolution time from 3 weeks to 2 days.

"The pricing automation was transformational. Our team can now focus on strategy instead of spreadsheets. Accuracy improved dramatically, and our invoicing process is much faster."

Director of Pricing B2B Software Company
