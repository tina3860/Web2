Data Quality Monitoring System

Built automated monitoring system detecting 95% of data quality issues within 15 minutes

The Business Problem

Poor data quality was silently corrupting downstream analytics and causing incorrect business decisions. Data validation was manual and infrequent, with quality issues often not discovered until they impacted reports or generated customer complaints. The team had no systematic way to detect anomalies.

Leadership needed proactive data quality monitoring rather than reactive issue discovery.

Findings

Root cause analysis revealed:

  • No standardized data quality metrics or thresholds defined
  • Manual weekly quality spot-checks missing 85% of issues
  • Quality issues propagating through 5-6 downstream processes before detection
  • No alerting mechanism to notify teams of emerging problems

Results

  • Defined 28 data quality rules across all critical datasets
  • Implemented automated monitoring with continuous validation
  • Detected 95% of quality issues within 15 minutes of occurrence
  • Created tiered alerting system notifying appropriate teams immediately
  • Prevented 45+ data-driven business decisions based on bad data
  • Reduced time to remediation from 3-5 days to same-day resolution

Immediate Impact on Operations

The organization now has confidence in data quality. Data engineers can prioritize fixes based on business impact. Analytics teams deliver reports with higher accuracy, reducing stakeholder questions about data validity.
