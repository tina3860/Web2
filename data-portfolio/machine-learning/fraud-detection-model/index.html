Fraud Detection Model

Real-time fraud detection blocking 94% of fraudulent transactions with minimal false positives

The Business Problem

The payment processing company was losing $3.2 million annually to fraudulent transactions. Manual fraud reviews caused 18% of legitimate transactions to be incorrectly blocked, frustrating customers. The fraud team had no real-time detection capability.

Leadership needed machine learning to detect fraud patterns in real-time while minimizing customer friction.

Findings

Fraud pattern analysis revealed:

  • Geographic anomalies in transaction locations vs. customer history
  • Velocity patterns (rapid sequence of transactions) indicating compromised accounts
  • Purchase amount deviations from customer baseline behavior
  • Merchant category combinations suspiciously concentrated in fraud cases

Results

  • Deployed real-time gradient boosting model for transaction scoring
  • Detected 94% of fraudulent transactions blocking $3.8M in fraud
  • Achieved 2.1% false positive rate (legitimate transactions declined)
  • Reduced customer service fraud inquiry volume by 67%
  • Implemented explainable model providing clear decision rationale
  • Integrated model into payment gateway for <500ms decision latency

Immediate Impact on Operations

Fraud team now reviews 80% fewer transactions, focusing on high-risk patterns. Customer complaints about blocked transactions dropped 71%. The company protected revenue while maintaining excellent customer experience.
