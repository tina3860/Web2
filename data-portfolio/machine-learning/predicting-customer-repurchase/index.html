Predicting Customer Repurchase

View Code View Documentation

The Business Problem

Sales data was siloed across multiple systems, making it difficult to generate unified reports and analyze performance trends. Manual data consolidation led to errors and delayed insights for sales leadership.

The company needed a robust, centralized database to enable real-time analytics and support self-service reporting for business users.

Findings

Key findings included:

  • Data sources lacked consistent keys, requiring a master data management approach
  • Existing schema did not support multi-dimensional analysis (e.g., by region, product, rep)
  • Performance bottlenecks in current reporting due to inefficient queries and lack of indexing

Solution

I designed a star schema sales analytics database, integrating data from CRM, ERP, and e-commerce platforms. The new model included dimension tables for products, customers, sales reps, and time, enabling flexible slicing and dicing of sales metrics.

  • Implemented ETL pipelines to automate data ingestion and transformation
  • Added indexes and partitioning to optimize query performance
  • Developed documentation and data dictionary for business users

Impact

The new database reduced reporting time from hours to seconds, empowered business users with self-service analytics, and improved data quality and trust in sales metrics.
