Recommendation Engine

ML-based personalized recommendations increasing cross-sell revenue by 26%

The Business Problem

The e-commerce platform used a basic "customers also bought" approach to recommendations, missing personalization opportunities. Cross-sell revenue was stagnant while industry competitors were achieving 30%+ revenue uplift through targeted recommendations.

The business needed sophisticated recommendation algorithms to increase average order value and customer lifetime value.

Findings

Recommendation analysis discovered:

  • Distinct product affinity clusters based on customer demographics
  • Browsing behavior predicting purchase intent more accurately than purchase history
  • Seasonal variations in product relevance requiring dynamic recommendations
  • Collaborative filtering patterns revealing hidden customer segments

Results

  • Deployed hybrid recommendation system combining collaborative and content-based filtering
  • Increased cross-sell revenue by 26% ($1.8M annually)
  • Improved click-through rate on recommendations from 2.1% to 4.8%
  • Boosted average order value by $12 per transaction
  • Enabled personalized recommendations across 5 touchpoints (homepage, product page, email, cart, checkout)
  • Achieved recommendation relevance matching manual curation while serving real-time

Immediate Impact on Operations

Merchandising team iterates faster using data-driven insights into customer preferences. Customers receive highly relevant recommendations improving satisfaction. The increased cross-sell revenue compounds customer lifetime value.

"The recommendation system is a revenue multiplier for us. Customers are buying more and are happier receiving relevant suggestions. The system improves continuously as it learns more about preferences."

Chief Revenue Officer E-Commerce Company
