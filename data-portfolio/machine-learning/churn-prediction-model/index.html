Churn Prediction Model

Developed ML model predicting customer churn with 87% accuracy, enabling proactive retention

The Business Problem

The SaaS company experienced 8% monthly churn affecting growth targets. Sales team had no visibility into which customers were at risk, making proactive retention impossible. Without predictive capability, interventions were reactive and ineffective.

Leadership needed early warning signals to identify and engage at-risk customers before cancellation.

Findings

Historical analysis of churned customers revealed:

  • Customers reducing feature usage 30 days before cancellation
  • Support ticket patterns changing 20 days pre-churn
  • Contract value declining in quarters preceding cancellation
  • Renewal rate predictable from engagement metrics

Results

  • Built predictive model achieving 87% accuracy in churn prediction
  • Identified 450 at-risk customers with 60+ days to projected churn
  • Enabled targeted retention campaigns with 28% success rate
  • Recovered $1.2 million annual revenue from prevented cancellations
  • Created early warning dashboard for sales leadership
  • Reduced average days-to-churn detection from reactive to proactive window

Immediate Impact on Operations

Sales team now receives weekly at-risk customer lists enabling targeted engagement. Retention marketing team focuses on high-confidence churn predictions, improving campaign effectiveness.

"The churn model has been a game-changer for our retention strategy. Predicting which customers are at risk allows us to intervene before they leave. The financial impact has been immediate and substantial."

VP of Customer Success SaaS Company
