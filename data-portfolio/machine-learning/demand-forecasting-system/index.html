Demand Forecasting System

ML-powered demand forecasting improving inventory accuracy by 31% and reducing waste by 22%

The Business Problem

The retail organization's inventory forecasting relied on manual judgment and simple trend analysis, causing stockouts during high-demand periods and excess inventory during downturns. Over $2 million annually was lost to aged inventory write-offs and missed sales.

Supply chain leadership needed more accurate demand signals to optimize purchasing decisions and inventory levels.

Findings

Demand analysis uncovered:

  • Strong seasonal patterns varying by product category
  • Weather dependencies affecting demand for seasonal items
  • Promotional events creating demand spikes 2-3 weeks in advance
  • Competitor activity influencing customer switching behavior

Results

  • Deployed ensemble ML model incorporating 45+ demand signals
  • Achieved 88% MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error) across all categories
  • Improved inventory accuracy from 79% to 93% (31% improvement)
  • Reduced aged inventory write-offs by 22% ($440,000 annual savings)
  • Decreased stockout events by 18% improving customer satisfaction
  • Enabled data-driven inventory replenishment decisions

Immediate Impact on Operations

Inventory team uses model-generated replenishment recommendations, reducing ordering time by 40%. Supply chain now carries optimal inventory levels, freeing up $600,000 in working capital previously tied up in excess stock.

"The demand forecasting system has transformed our inventory management. We're staffing better, carrying less excess inventory, and serving customers better by reducing stockouts. The financial impact has been immediate."
