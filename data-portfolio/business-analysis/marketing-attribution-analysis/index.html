Marketing Attribution Analysis

Built multi-touch attribution model improving marketing ROI clarity and allocation accuracy

The Business Problem

The marketing organization used last-click attribution to measure campaign effectiveness, vastly overvaluing direct traffic and undervaluing top-of-funnel campaigns. Budget allocation decisions were based on incomplete understanding of customer journey, leading to misallocation of $500K+ annually.

Leadership needed sophisticated attribution modeling to understand true impact of each marketing channel.

Findings

Attribution analysis revealed:

  • Average customer journey was 5.2 touchpoints across 3+ channels before conversion
  • Organic search received 80% of last-click credit but accounted for only 20% of actual value creation
  • Paid social and email were undervalued despite strong middle-funnel impact
  • Channel synergies existed that last-click couldn't capture

Results

  • Built multi-touch attribution model using time-decay weighting
  • Analyzed 50,000+ complete customer journeys across 6 marketing channels
  • Revealed true ROI by channel: paid search 2.8x, organic 1.2x, paid social 3.1x
  • Identified optimal channel mix maximizing overall marketing ROI
  • Recommended budget reallocation delivering 18% marketing ROI improvement
  • Created ongoing attribution monitoring dashboard

Immediate Impact on Operations

Marketing team can now allocate budget according to true channel impact rather than last-click illusions. Campaign mix optimization increased overall marketing efficiency by 18%, demonstrating value of sophisticated attribution analysis.

"The attribution analysis completely changed how we think about marketing ROI. We were over-investing in certain channels and under-investing in others. With proper attribution, our marketing efficiency improved significantly."
