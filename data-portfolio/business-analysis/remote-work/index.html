Remote Work & Business Well-Being

View Code View Documentation

The Business Problem

The COVID-19 pandemic forced a rapid shift to remote work, leaving businesses without frameworks for evaluating whether remote or hybrid policies were sustainable.

Companies needed actionable insights to determine whether remote work was appropriate for their context.

Findings

Key findings included:

  • Remote work increased job satisfaction and work-life balance across most industries
  • Collaboration difficulties and isolation were the most cited challenges
  • Tech, finance, and education were the most viable industries for remote work
  • Gen Z reported higher isolation while Millennials reported higher productivity gains
  • Cost savings for both employers and employees were significant and consistent

Results

After merging and cleaning survey data across two years — standardizing columns and creating generational cohorts — clear behavioral patterns emerged across industries, role types, and generations.

These findings were translated into actionable recommendations for businesses evaluating remote and hybrid policies, supported by visualizations designed to communicate insights clearly to non-technical stakeholders.

Impact

TThe research gave businesses a data-driven framework for evaluating remote work suitability based on industry, role type, and team composition..
