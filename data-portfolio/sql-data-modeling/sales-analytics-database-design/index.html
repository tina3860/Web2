Sales Analytics Database Design

Designing a scalable relational database to track sales performance, inventory, and customer behavior across multiple business units.

The Business Problem

The organization had sales data scattered across multiple legacy systems with inconsistent data formats. The sales team spent 30% of their time manually consolidating reports instead of analyzing trends and making strategic decisions. Limited data visibility meant they couldn't quickly identify top-performing products, underperforming regions, or customer segments at risk of churn.

They needed a centralized, reliable database that could handle complex queries and provide real-time insights into sales performance.

Findings

After analyzing the existing systems, I discovered:

  • Data was siloed across 4 different systems with overlapping information
  • No consistent customer IDs meant duplicate records and data inconsistency
  • Historical data was scattered with no audit trail
  • Query response times exceeded 30 minutes for simple reports

Results

I designed and implemented a normalized relational database with the following outcomes:

  • 60% time savings: Automated reporting reduced manual consolidation from 30% to 12% of workflow
  • Real-time insights: Query response times improved from 30 minutes to <5 seconds
  • Data accuracy: Eliminated duplicate records and established single source of truth
  • Scalability: Database handles 10x growth without performance degradation

Immediate Impact on Operations

The sales team immediately gained visibility into:

  • Regional sales performance with daily automated reports
  • Product profitability with margin analysis
  • Customer lifetime value predictions
  • Inventory movement patterns by territory

Leaders could now make data-driven decisions in real-time, and the analytics team could focus on strategic analysis instead of data wrangling.
