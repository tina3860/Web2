HR Analytics Platform

Developed HR analytics dashboards enabling data-driven people management decisions

The Business Problem

The HR department tracked employee data in disconnected systems and spreadsheets, making it impossible to answer basic questions about workforce composition, turnover, or diversity. Leadership had minimal visibility into talent pipeline, retention risks, or compensation equity.

HR needed centralized people analytics to guide strategic workforce planning and identify talent risks early.

Findings

HR data audit uncover:

  • Employee data scattered across HRIS, payroll, and recruiting systems
  • No historical tracking of headcount, hiring rates, or turnover
  • Limited visibility into compensation distribution across demographic groups
  • No structured approach to identifying flight risks

Results

  • Built comprehensive HR analytics platform consolidating all employee data
  • Created 15+ dashboards covering workforce planning, recruitment, retention, compensation
  • Identified 8 high-risk flights (50% of departures the following quarter)
  • Enabled compensation equity analysis by department, role, and demographics
  • Tracked turnover by cohort identifying high-risk periods
  • Provided predictive staffing requirements based on headcount trends

Immediate Impact on Operations

HR team can proactively identify at-risk employees enabling retention interventions. Recruiting priorities are now data-driven based on actual workforce gaps. Compensation decisions have better visibility into equity across the organization.

"The HR analytics platform has transformed how we think about workforce management. We know where our talent risks are, we can predict staffing needs, and we have better visibility into compensation equity. It's incredibly valuable."

VP of Human Resources Technology Company
