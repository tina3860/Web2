Sales Pipeline Analysis

Created sales analytics dashboards improving pipeline visibility and forecast accuracy

The Business Problem

The sales organization couldn't accurately forecast quarterly revenue due to inconsistent pipeline data entry and visibility challenges. CRM data was unreliable, with sales reps updating deals infrequently or inaccurately. Management had no clear view of sales health or progress toward targets.

Sales leadership needed trustworthy data to make decisions about resource allocation and pipeline management.

Findings

CRM audit revealed:

  • Inconsistent deal stage definitions across sales team regions
  • 30% of deals missing critical fields (decision date, close probability)
  • No historical tracking of pipeline progression
  • Manual quarterly forecasting with high variance from actuals

Results

  • Built Excel/Power Query pipeline analytics covering 250+ active deals
  • Created data quality rules flagging incomplete pipeline records
  • Developed historical pipeline tracking enabling trend analysis
  • Built sales forecast dashboard with predicted revenue by region and product
  • Improved forecast accuracy from 68% to 92% (24-point improvement)
  • Enabled weekly rather than monthly pipeline visibility

Immediate Impact on Operations

Sales leadership can now confidently forecast quarterly revenue with higher accuracy. Sales managers have visibility into deal progression enabling early intervention. Territory management decisions are now data-driven with clear pipeline health metrics.

"The pipeline analytics give us confidence that we understand our business. Forecasting is much more accurate now, and we can identify problem deals early before they stall."

VP of Sales Enterprise Software Company
