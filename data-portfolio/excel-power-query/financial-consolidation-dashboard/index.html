Financial Consolidation Dashboard

Built Power BI dashboard consolidating 8 business units into unified financial reporting

The Business Problem

The multi-unit financial organization manually consolidated reports from 8 business divisions, requiring 3 days of effort to produce monthly consolidated financials. Each unit reported in different formats and chart of accounts structures, creating reconciliation challenges and inconsistent reporting.

Finance leadership needed a unified, real-time view of consolidated financial performance across all units.

Findings

Consolidation audit revealed:

  • 8 different accounting systems with misaligned account hierarchies
  • Manual account mapping errors causing reconciliation variance
  • No automated elimination entries for inter-company transactions
  • Currency conversion done manually with outdated exchange rates

Results

  • Built Power BI model consolidating all 8 business units
  • Created automated inter-company transaction elimination logic
  • Implemented currency conversion with real-time exchange rates
  • Reduced consolidation time from 3 days to 1 hour per month
  • Achieved perfect reconciliation between source systems and consolidated reports
  • Enabled month-end close process to complete 5 days earlier

Immediate Impact on Operations

Finance team eliminated 3 days monthly of manual consolidation work. Month-end close accelerated by 5 days enabling earlier financial reporting. Executives now have same-day visibility into consolidated performance.

"The consolidated dashboard means we can close the books 5 days sooner, which is huge for our financial planning. The accuracy boost and elimination of manual work have been game-changing."

Chief Financial Officer Multi-Unit Organization
